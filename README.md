# Welcome to Dino 3D!

Get ready to run and jump in this 3D recreation of the classic Chrome Dino game. We've brought the prehistoric desert to life with a fully immersive, three-dimensional world. Your goal is simple: survive as long as you can!

Run as a swift raptor, leap over obstacles like cacti and ancient pyramids, and challenge your friends to beat your high score. The game gets faster the longer you play, so stay sharp and keep your reflexes honed.

Are you ready to face the challenge?

Play the game now: https://dino-threejs.vercel.app
